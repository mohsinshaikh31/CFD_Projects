## Analytical Validation

The theoretical solution was obtained using ideal-gas, quasi-one-dimensional isentropic-flow relations.

### 1. Area Ratio

$$
A_t = \frac{\pi D_t^2}{4}
$$

$$
A_e = \frac{\pi D_e^2}{4}
$$

$$
\frac{A_e}{A_t}=2.25
$$

### 2. Exit Mach Number

$$
M_e \approx 2.20
$$

### 3. Exit Temperature

$$
T_e =
\frac{T_0}
{1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_e^2}
$$

$$
T_e \approx 152.4\ K
$$

### 4. Exit Velocity

$$
a_e=\sqrt{\gamma R T_e}
$$

$$
V_e=M_ea_e
$$

$$
V_e\approx544.5\ m/s
$$

### 5. Exit Pressure

$$
\frac{P_e}{P_0}
=
\left[
1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_e^2
\right]^{-\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}}
$$

$$
\frac{P_e}{P_0}\approx0.0935
$$
